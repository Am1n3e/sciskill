Science Skills is a collection by **Amine ElHattami** for work around research: auditing citations, organizing reviewer concerns, and planning evidence-grounded responses.

The tools perform repeatable operations and make their evidence inspectable. The agent skill defines an analysis procedure. Researchers retain responsibility for scientific interpretation, new experiments, and final responses.

The collection includes bib-lint, Rebuttal Desk, and the SciSkill rebuttal-analysis instructions. See the [source repository](https://github.com/Am1n3e/sciskill) and [author profile](https://github.com/Am1n3e).
