Science Skills is a collection by **Amine ElHattami**, beginning with reference auditing and review-response planning.

The premise is practical: agents can help with the work around research while scientists retain responsibility for the questions, interpretations, and decisions. A useful capability should have a clear purpose, repeatable steps, and outputs with their evidence attached.

## A tool, a workflow, an inspectable result

The tool performs repeatable operations. The agent skill explains when and how to use it, how to interpret its output, and where to stop for human judgment. A viewer or report makes the result inspectable.

bib-lint is the first public tool. Rebuttal Desk is an existing local prototype. Reusable collection-wide skills are still being developed.

## A growing catalog

Future additions will be organized around concrete research tasks. The collection does not claim to automate scientific discovery or replace domain expertise. Each capability should describe its availability, evidence, limits, and evaluation status.
